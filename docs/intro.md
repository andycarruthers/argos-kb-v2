# Welcome to the Argos Knowledge Base

This is a **prototype** rebuild of the Argos knowledge base — organised around what you're trying to *do*, not around a screen-by-screen list of what each button does.

:::info Prototype
This covers 24 topics — the original 7 highest-deflection support topics, plus the full 20-article set from Argos's live "Argos KB — Article Pipeline" review in Notion (a few of which were merged into existing articles rather than published separately, and one spun out into its own article where a reviewer felt it didn't belong with the rest). Every pipeline article carries the real corrections its reviewers gave it. The rest of the existing knowledge base is still available through your usual support channel while this is being built out.
:::

## Where to start

- **New to Argos, or setting up a new team member?** Start with [Access & Security](/new-user-access-setup).
- **Numbers not matching between systems?** Go straight to [Diagnosing a GL/Cashbook balance variance](/balance-variance-diagnosis).
- **Running your month-end close?** See [Running month-end](/month-end-process).
- **Ending a loan, lease, or dealer facility early?** See [Working out an early termination or payout figure](/early-termination-process).

Use the search bar (top right) if you know roughly what you're looking for, or browse by category in the sidebar.

## How these articles are built

Every article here is grounded in real, anonymised patterns from actual support tickets — not just a rewrite of the old manual. Where something in an article is still being confirmed with an Argos subject matter expert before we're fully confident in it, you'll see a note like this:

:::caution Under review
Parts of this article are still being confirmed internally.
:::

If you see one of those and something doesn't match your experience, please get in touch with support — that's exactly the kind of feedback this rebuild needs.
